## 📝 Creating Your First vCard

### Step 1: Basic Information

Fill in your essential details:

- **Name** - Your full name (e.g., "John Smith")
- **Company Name** - Your company or organization
- **Email** - Your primary email address
- **Phone** - Your primary phone number
- **Mobile** - Your mobile number (optional)
- **Address** - Your business address (Street, City, State, ZIP, Country)

> **💡 Tip**: The email you enter will be used for lead notifications and as the sender for automated emails.

### Step 2: Visual Customization

Make your card visually appealing:

1. **Upload Profile Photo**
   - Recommended size: 400x400px or larger (square)
   - Formats: JPG, PNG
   - This appears prominently on your card

2. **Upload Banner Image**
   - Recommended size: 1200x400px (wide format)
   - Formats: JPG, PNG
   - This is the header image on your card

3. **Choose Colors**
   - **Primary Color**: Background color (default: White)
   - **Secondary Color**: Accent color for buttons and highlights (default: #4C75A3)
   - Click the color picker to choose your brand colors

> **💡 Tip**: Use your company's brand colors for consistency!

### Step 3: Choose Your Template

Vinc offers **5 professional templates**:

1. **Classic** - Traditional, clean layout with centered profile
2. **Modern** - Contemporary design with side-by-side layout
3. **Corporate** - Professional business-focused template
4. **Minimal** - Clean, tabbed navigation design
5. **Creative** - Playful design with floating elements

**How to choose**: Click through the templates in the form to see a preview, or select one that matches your style.

### Step 4: Set Your Website URL (Slug) ⚠️ CRITICAL STEP

- **Website Slug** - This creates your unique URL
  - Example: If your slug is `john-smith`, your card will be at: `https://yourdomain.com/john-smith`
  - **Auto-generated**: The slug is automatically created from your name when you type it
  - **Customizable**: You can edit it to make it shorter or more memorable
  - **Unique**: Each slug must be unique - if yours is taken, Vinc will suggest an alternative

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT - Read This Before Programming NFC Cards!**
> 
> - **Set your slug properly** - This becomes your permanent URL for your vCard
> - **Preview slugs are temporary** - During the creation process, Vinc assigns a temporary preview slug (like `preview-2`) for live preview purposes
> - **Your final slug** is what you set in Step 2 of the wizard - this is your permanent URL
> - **Always verify your slug** before programming NFC cards - make sure it's the permanent slug, not the preview slug
> - **Use your permanent slug URL** for NFC cards: `https://yourdomain.com/{your-permanent-slug}`
> - **Keep it short and memorable** - Use your name or a professional handle

> **💡 Tip**: Your slug becomes your permanent URL. Choose wisely - you can change it later, but it's better to get it right the first time! If you're planning to use NFC cards, double-check your slug is set correctly before programming.

**Before programming your NFC card, you MUST set your website slug properly:**

1. **During Card Creation** (in `/get-started` wizard):
   - Your slug is auto-generated from your name

2. **After Card Creation**:
   - Go to your vCard record in **Vinc** → **Cards**
   - Edit the **"Website Slug"** field
   - Click **"Generate Website"** to update

3. **Preview Slug Note**:
   - During the creation process, you may see a preview slug (like `preview-2`)
   - This is temporary and only used for live preview
   - Your final slug will be the one you set in the form
   - Make sure to set your permanent slug before programming NFC cards!

> **🔑 Key Point**: Your NFC card should point to your **permanent vCard URL**, not the preview URL. Always verify your slug is set correctly before programming!

### How NFC Works

**Two Options for NFC Programming:**

#### Option A: Universal URL (For New Users/Onboarding)
- **URL**: `https://yourdomain.com/get-started`
- **Use Case**: When you want people to create their own cards
- **When Someone Taps**:
  - New users → Onboarding wizard to create their card
  - Existing users → Direct to their card dashboard
  - Visitors → Can access public cards or create their own

#### Option B: Your Personal vCard URL (Recommended)
- **URL**: `https://yourdomain.com/{your-slug}` (e.g., `https://yourdomain.com/john-smith`)
- **Use Case**: When you want people to see YOUR specific card
- **When Someone Taps**: They go directly to your vCard page
- **Best For**: Business cards, badges, marketing materials

> **💡 Recommendation**: Use Option B (your personal vCard URL) for business cards and networking. Use Option A (`/get-started`) for onboarding new team members.

### Step-by-Step NFC Programming Guide

#### Step 1: Get Your vCard URL

1. **Create your card** at `https://yourdomain.com/get-started` (make sure to set your slug!)
2. **After creation**, your card URL will be: `https://yourdomain.com/{your-slug}`
3. **Copy this URL** - you'll need it to program your NFC card

**Or use the built-in NFC Setup Guide:**
- Go to your vCard record → Click **"NFC Setup Guide"** button
- Or visit: `https://yourdomain.com/nfc/setup/{your-vcard-id}`
- The guide will show your exact URL and walk you through programming

👉 **Direct Link to NFC Setup Guide**: `https://yourdomain.com/nfc/setup/{your-vcard-id}` (replace `{your-vcard-id}` with your vCard ID number)

#### Step 2: Install NFC Programming App

Download a free NFC programming app on your phone:

**For iPhone (iPhone 7 or newer):**
- **NFC Tools** - [Download from App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nfc-tools/id1252962749)
- Enable NFC in Settings → NFC (usually enabled by default on iPhone 7+)

**For Android:**
- **NFC Tools** - [Download from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wakdev.wdnfc)
- Enable NFC in Settings → Connections → NFC

**Alternative Apps:**
- TagWriter (NXP)
- NFC TagInfo
- Any NFC programming app that supports URL/URI records

#### Step 3: Program Your NFC Card

1. **Open NFC Tools** app on your phone
2. **Tap "Write"** (or "Write Tags" depending on app)
3. **Add a Record** → Select **"URI/URL"** or **"URL"**
4. **Enter Your vCard URL**:
   - For your personal card: `https://yourdomain.com/{your-slug}`
   - For universal onboarding: `https://yourdomain.com/get-started`
5. **Tap "Write"** or **"OK"**
6. **Hold your phone** over the NFC card/band
7. **Wait for confirmation** - You'll see "Write successful" or similar message

#### Step 4: Test Your NFC Card

1. **Test immediately** after programming:
   - Tap the NFC card with your phone
   - It should open your vCard URL in the browser
   - Verify it goes to the correct page

2. **Test with different devices**:
   - Try with iPhone and Android
   - Make sure it works for others, not just you

3. **If it doesn't work**:
   - Check that NFC is enabled on your phone
   - Verify the URL was written correctly
   - Try reprogramming the card
   - Check that your vCard website is published

### NFC Programming Tips

- **Write Once**: Most NFC cards can only be written once (read-only after programming)
- **Test Before Distributing**: Always test your NFC card before giving it to others
- **Keep URL Short**: Shorter URLs are easier to program and more reliable
- **Use HTTPS**: Always use `https://` in your URL for security
- **Check Compatibility**: Ensure your NFC card is compatible with your programming app
- **Backup URL**: Write down your URL somewhere safe in case you need to reprogram

### ⚠️ Changing Your Slug - Keep Your Hardware in Sync!

**What Happens When You Change Your Slug?**

When you change your website slug (URL), your card gets a **new URL**:
- **Old URL**: `https://yourdomain.com/old-slug` → **Stops working** ❌
- **New URL**: `https://yourdomain.com/new-slug` → **Becomes active** ✅

**Important Steps After Changing Your Slug:**

1. **Click "Generate Website"** (Required!)
   - Go to your vCard record → **Vinc** → **Cards** → Select your card
   - Edit the **"Website Slug"** field
   - **Click "Generate Website"** button to activate the new URL
   - The old URL will no longer work

2. **Update Your NFC Cards** (If you have any)
   - **Reprogram all NFC cards** with the new URL
   - Old NFC cards pointing to the old slug will **not work** anymore
   - Use the NFC Setup Guide to get your new URL: `https://yourdomain.com/nfc/setup/{your-vcard-id}`
   - Follow the programming steps again with the new URL

3. **Update Your QR Codes** (If you have printed ones)
   - **Generate a new QR code** with the updated URL
   - Download and reprint any business cards, badges, or marketing materials
   - Old QR codes will still work but point to a broken link

4. **Update Shared Links**
   - Update any links you've shared (email signatures, social media, websites)
   - Notify people who have your old link bookmarked

**Best Practice:**
- **Set your slug correctly the first time** - Avoid changing it if possible
- **If you must change it**: Plan ahead and update all your materials at once
- **Test the new URL** before reprogramming NFC cards
- **Keep a list** of where you've used your card URL (NFC cards, printed materials, etc.)

> **🔑 Key Point**: Changing your slug is like changing your address - everything pointing to the old address stops working. Make sure to update all your hardware (NFC cards) and materials (QR codes) when you change it!

## 🔄 Bulk Onboarding (For Admins)

Bulk onboarding allows you to create multiple user accounts and vCards at once by uploading a CSV or Excel file. Perfect for onboarding entire teams, departments, or organizations.

### Overview

**What Bulk Onboarding Does:**
- Creates user accounts for multiple people at once
- Sends personalized activation emails with magic links
- Pre-creates vCards with common information you provide
- Tracks progress and completion status
- Sends you a summary email when complete

**Who Can Use It:**
- System administrators
- Users with ERP Manager permissions
- Users with System Administrator group

### Step-by-Step Process

#### Step 1: Access Bulk Onboarding

1. Go to: `https://yourdomain.com/bulk-onboard`
   - Or click the **"Bulk Onboarding"** button in your dashboard
   - Or navigate to **Vinc** → **Cards** → **Bulk Onboarding** (if available)

#### Step 2: Download the Template

1. **Download CSV Template**: 
   - Click the **"Download template CSV"** link on the bulk onboarding page
   - Or go directly to: `https://yourdomain.com/bulk-onboard/template`
   - The template includes:
     - **Row 1**: Explanations for each column
     - **Row 2**: Column headers
     - **Rows 3-7**: Example data to guide you

2. **Template Columns Available**:
   - `name` (required) - Full name of the user
   - `email` (required) - Email address (must be unique)
   - `phone` (optional) - Phone number
   - `mobile` (optional) - Mobile number
   - `function` (optional) - Job title or function
   - `about` (optional) - About text (overrides default)
   - `street`, `street2`, `city`, `zip` (optional) - Address fields
   - `country` (optional) - Country name (e.g., "United States")
   - `state` (optional) - State/Province name (e.g., "California")
   - `linkedin_url`, `facebook_url`, `twitter_url`, `instagram_url`, `whatsapp_url`, `youtube_url` (optional) - Social media links
   - `calendly_url` (optional) - Calendar booking link
   - `website_slug` (optional) - Custom URL slug (auto-generated if not provided)

#### Step 3: Prepare Your User List

1. **Open the Template** in Excel, Google Sheets, or any CSV editor
2. **Fill in User Data**:
   - **Minimum Required**: Name and Email for each user
   - **Recommended**: Add phone, function, and address for complete profiles
   - **Optional**: Add social media links, custom slugs, etc.
3. **Save as CSV** (UTF-8 encoding recommended)

**Example Data:**
```
name,email,phone,function
John Smith,john.smith@company.com,555-0100,Sales Manager
Jane Doe,jane.doe@company.com,555-0101,Marketing Director
```

#### Step 4: Create a New Batch

1. Click **"Create New Batch"** or go to: `https://yourdomain.com/bulk-onboard/new`
2. **Fill in Common Information** (applied to all users):
   - **Batch Name** - Name for this batch (e.g., "Q1 2025 Onboarding")
   - **Company Name** - Applied to all vCards
   - **Default Banner Image** - Upload a banner that all cards will use
   - **Default Profile Photo** - Upload a default profile photo (users can change this)
   - **Primary Color** - Background color for all cards
   - **Secondary Color** - Accent color for buttons
   - **Website Template** - Choose Classic, Modern, Corporate, Minimal, or Creative
   - **About Text** - Default about section (users can customize)
   - **Lead Collection Settings** - Enable lead forms, set button labels, etc.
   - **Social Media Links** - Default links (users can add their own)
   - **Other Settings** - Reviews, services, etc.

3. **Upload Your CSV/Excel File**:
   - Click "Choose File" or drag and drop
   - Supported formats: CSV, XLSX (Excel)
   - Maximum file size: Check your Odoo configuration

4. **Review Preview**:
   - See how many users will be created
   - Preview the data that will be imported
   - Check for any validation errors

5. **Submit the Batch**:
   - Click **"Start Bulk Onboarding"**
   - Processing begins immediately in the background

#### Step 5: Track Progress

1. **View Batch List**: 
   - Go to `https://yourdomain.com/bulk-onboard`
   - See all batches with their status

2. **Batch Statuses**:
   - **Draft** - Created but not started
   - **Processing** - Currently creating accounts
   - **Completed** - All users processed
   - **Failed** - Errors occurred (check details)

3. **View Batch Details**:
   - Click on a batch to see detailed progress
   - View statistics:
     - **Total Users** - Number of users in the batch
     - **Invited** - Emails sent successfully
     - **Activated** - Users who clicked activation link
     - **Completed** - Users who finished setting up their card
     - **Failed** - Users with errors (with error messages)

4. **Real-Time Updates**:
   - Page auto-refreshes to show progress
   - See individual user status
   - Check which users need attention

#### Step 6: Resend Invitations

If some users didn't receive or lost their activation email:

1. Go to the batch details page
2. Find users with "Invited" or "Failed" status
3. Click **"Resend Invitation"** for individual users
4. Or **"Resend All Failed"** to resend to all failed users

#### Activation Process

1. **User Clicks Magic Link**:
   - Taken to activation page: `https://yourdomain.com/bulk-onboard/activate?token={unique-token}`
   - Link is unique and secure (expires in 7 days)

2. **Set Password**:
   - User creates their password
   - No password needed to activate (passwordless activation)

3. **Account Activated**:
   - User account is created
   - vCard is pre-populated with:
     - Common data you provided (company, colors, template, etc.)
     - Individual data from CSV (name, email, phone, etc.)
     - Default profile photo and banner (if provided)

4. **User Can Customize**:
   - User logs in to Odoo
   - Goes to their vCard
   - Can customize all fields
   - Can upload their own profile photo
   - Can add personal content

### Completion Notification

- **Summary Statistics**:
  - Total users processed
  - Successfully invited
  - Activated accounts
  - Completed cards
  - Failed users (with reasons)
- **Link to View Batch** - Click to see full details

### Troubleshooting

**Users Not Receiving Emails:**
- Check email server configuration in Odoo
- Verify email addresses are correct
- Check spam/junk folders
- Resend invitation from batch page

**Activation Links Expired:**
- Links expire after 7 days
- Resend invitation to get a new link
- User can request a new link if needed

**Users Can't Log In:**
- Ensure they clicked the activation link first
- Check that they're using the correct email
- Verify their account was created successfully

**Batch Stuck in "Processing":**
- Check server logs for errors
- Refresh the batch page
- Contact support if it's stuck for more than 30 minutes

## 🆘 Need Help?

### Common Questions

**Q: How do I change my card URL?**
A: Edit the "Website Slug" field in your vCard form, click "Generate Website" again, and **reprogram any NFC cards** with the new URL. See "Changing Your Slug" section above for full details.

**Q: Can I have multiple cards?**
A: Yes! You can create as many cards as you need. Just go to **Vinc** → **Cards** → **Create**.

**Q: How do I update my card after it's live?**
A: Edit your vCard record, make changes, and click "Generate Website" to update the live version.

**Q: Can I customize the email templates?**
A: Yes! In your vCard form, you can customize intro emails and lead-back emails with HTML templates.

**Q: How do I track who viewed my card?**
A: View the "Page Views" count on your vCard dashboard. For detailed analytics, check the digest emails.

**Q: What if someone's email bounces?**
A: Odoo automatically handles bounces. After multiple bounces, contacts are marked as invalid.
